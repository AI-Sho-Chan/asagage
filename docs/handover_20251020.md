## Failure Modes and Mitigations
- **RSS columns blank or `#VALUE!`:** occurs when Excel auto-recovery or manual edits clear formulas while `SetColumnFormula` is blocked by protection, causing 1004 errors. Fix by keeping columns H-T locked (now enforced in VBA and `repair_realtime_formulas.py`) and re-running `repair_realtime_formulas.py` to restore formulas.
- **Workbook corruption after manual XML edits:** editing `sheet6.xml` directly risked malformed XML and caused Excel repair mode, deleting buttons. Resolution: abandon direct edits, restore from backup, and rely on COM scripts for formula installation.
